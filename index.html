✦ How I Work

Signal Lab

I move from ambiguity to a shippable experience through one clear loop: define the signal, make it tangible, then carry the intent through production.

01 / Frame

Define the signal before designing the surface.

I align the user need, business intent, technical constraints, and the decision the work must unlock. That shared frame keeps exploration purposeful without narrowing it too early.

OutputProblem frame · Success criteria · Prioritized risks
02 / Prototype

Turn the riskiest assumption into something testable.

I build the smallest convincing flow that exposes interaction, content, and system behavior. Feedback arrives while the cost of changing direction is still low.

OutputTestable flow · Interaction model · Evidence for the next decision
03 / Ship

Carry the intent through the final production detail.

I pair design with implementation, resolve edge cases, and validate accessibility, responsiveness, and performance. Shipping closes one loop and gives the next one better evidence.

OutputProduction-ready experience · QA confidence · Learning loop
